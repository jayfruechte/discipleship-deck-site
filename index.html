The Discipleship Deck

Help and contact

The Discipleship Deck is a free iPhone and iPad app for Fighter Groups: two to four believers who meet each week to read Scripture, talk through one question, rejoice, repent, remind each other of God’s promises, and pray. It follows Frontier Church’s 2025 Discipleship Deck, a card for every week of the year in four practices: scripture, solitude, sabbath and self-denial. It’s for any believer, at Frontier or anywhere else.

Need help?

  • Email jay.fruechte@gmail.com with what you tapped and what you saw. We usually answer within a few days.
  • Read the privacy policy: everything you type stays on your device.

Getting started

  • Setup asks for your city, your name, the rest of your group, and when and where you meet. One phone runs the app for the whole group.
  • How to Fight for Joy is an optional six-week start. After it, your group picks up the church’s card for the week.
  • This Week shows the week’s card. Before you meet: read, think through the question and pray. Tap Fight for Joy Together when your group gathers.

Common questions

Which card are we on?
The deck follows the calendar: card 12 is week 12 of the year, so every group is on the same card. Weeks 47 to 52 have no card; the group still gathers, just without a question. Missed a week? Open it in the Deck tab and tap Run Week.
Do I need an account or internet?
No account. The Bible is stored on your device, so the app works offline. With internet, the week’s reading comes in the ESV.
How do I add the widgets?
Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Discipleship Deck. There are three: Fighter Group, Weekly Challenge and Prayer Requests.
I’m not getting reminders before we meet.
Check your meeting time in Group → Manage Group, then allow notifications in the app’s Settings → Notification Settings.
Can I move my group to a new phone?
Your group lives on your device. Restoring your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup brings it along.
How do I start over?
Settings → Start Over. Start a New Group keeps you and Our Journey; Erase Everything clears it all.
How is the app paid for?
It’s free, with no ads, supported by giving to Frontier Church. Give opens Frontier’s giving page in Safari.
