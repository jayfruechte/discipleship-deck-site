The Discipleship Deck: Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Last updated October 27, 2026

The Discipleship Deck is a free iPhone and iPad app from Frontier Church in Des Moines, Iowa. It helps Fighter Groups work through the Discipleship Deck together. This policy explains what the app keeps on your device, the small amount it sends, and why. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Frontier Church.

The short version

  • Everything you type stays on your device. That includes names, prayer requests, answers and notes.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • The app sends anonymous counts so Frontier Church can see where the deck is used and whether groups keep meeting. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • We never sell or share your information.

What stays on your device

Everything you write in the app is stored only on your iPhone or iPad. It is never sent to Frontier Church or to anyone else:

  • your name and the names of the people in your group
  • your group’s name, meeting time and meeting place
  • prayer requests, which ones were answered, and how God answered them
  • your answers to the card questions
  • Rejoice, Remind and Repent notes, journal entries, and notes on how a challenge went
  • your group’s history in Our Journey
  • photos you take of your capstone pages, and your group’s Survival Scripture Plan

If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup like any other app’s data. Apple protects that backup, and we can’t see it.

Reminders before your gathering, the “Pray for your group” reminder and the Live Activity during the gathering are set up on your device. Nothing in them is sent to us. The prayer reminder shows on your Lock Screen, so it names only the people with open requests (never what they asked for, and never private requests).

Widgets show your group’s week and open prayer requests on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, where anyone holding your device can see them. Requests you mark private are never shown on widgets.

Things you choose to share

Some buttons let you send something to other people yourself, such as an answered prayer or “Send it to the group” for the week’s challenge. These open your device’s share sheet, and you choose who receives it and which app sends it. The app doesn’t send anything until you do, and we never receive a copy. Private prayer requests can’t be shared.

TelemetryDeck also counts app sessions, the days the app is used and new installs. It receives the app version, the kind of device, the iOS version, your language and region, and a scrambled (hashed) device code it uses to count devices. That code can’t be traced back to you, isn’t shared with other apps, and isn’t used for advertising.

Never sent: names, photos, prayer requests, answers to the questions, notes or journal entries, your meeting time or place, or your group’s name.

Frontier Church sees only totals on TelemetryDeck’s dashboard, such as how many groups are in each city or how many gatherings happened this month. None of it is linked to who you are. It isn’t used for advertising, and it isn’t sold or shared with anyone else. The app doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, so it never asks for tracking permission.

TelemetryDeck may use the stats only to run its service for Frontier Church, and must protect them at least as well as this policy does. We keep them only as long as they help us see totals over time.

Turning it off: go to Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage with Frontier Church in the app. When it’s off, the app sends nothing to TelemetryDeck.

Scripture from the ESV API

Unless you choose the King James Version or your printed Bible (Settings → Bible), each week’s reading and meditation are shown in the English Standard Version. To get them, the app sends only the passage reference (for example “Romans 6:14”) to Crossway’s ESV API at api.esv.org. Nothing about you or your group goes with it. Crossway’s privacy policy is at crossway.org/policy. The full King James Bible is stored on your device, so Scripture works without internet. With the KJV or your printed Bible, nothing is sent to the ESV API.

Links out of the app

Give, Plan a Visit, Directions and the sermon links open in Safari or Maps. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Giving goes through Frontier Church’s Church Center page, and the app never sees your payment details.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing stats: Settings → Privacy, as described above.
  • Start fresh: Settings → Start a New Group clears the other people, their prayer requests, and the group’s name, time and place. Settings → Erase Everything clears everything you’ve entered (your answers about you, your Bible choice and your reminders too) and starts setup over; only your settings (like appearance and the stats choice) stay.
  • Remove it all: deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

Because the stats can’t be linked to a person, we have no way to find, show or delete one person’s stats. There is also no account to close.

Children

The app isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, we’ll update this page and its date before the new version of the app is released.
